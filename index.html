How to Play

Movement

WASD or Arrow Keys - Move your dragon

F - Toggle flight (uses stamina)

Exploration

Walk into objects to interact

Find gold, items, and enemies while exploring

Combat

SPACE - Attack (builds combo)

SHIFT+SPACE - Heavy Attack (more damage)

E - Special Ability (tribe-specific)

Q - Defend (reduces damage)

Other

I - Inventory | M - Map | H - Help

R - Rest (recover HP and stamina)
