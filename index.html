📖 Dragon Guide

🎮 Game Basics

🔍 Exploring

Click "Explore" to search your current location. You may find gold, items, healing herbs, or encounter enemies.

🗺️ Traveling

Use the Map or Travel button to move between kingdoms. Each location has different enemy levels and a unique boss.

💤 Resting

Rest to recover 40% of your HP and clear status effects. Use this between battles to stay healthy.

📜 Quests

Complete quests to earn XP, gold, and special items. Check your quest log regularly!

🎒 Inventory

Manage your items and equipment. Click items to use or equip them. Equipment boosts your stats!

⚔️ Combat Guide

⚔️ Attack

Basic attack. Builds combo for bonus damage.

💥 Heavy Attack

Deals 1.5x damage but leaves you briefly vulnerable.

✨ Special Ability

Your tribe's unique power. Has a 4-turn cooldown. Each tribe has different effects!

🛡️ Defend

Reduces incoming damage by 50%. Resets your combo.

🔥 Combo System

Chain attacks without defending or fleeing to build combo. Each combo level adds 10% bonus damage!

💥 Critical Hits

10% base chance to deal double damage. Increases with level (up to 30%).

🦠 Status Effects

🤢 Poison - Damage over time (5% HP/turn)
🔥 Burn - Higher damage over time (8% HP/turn)
❄️ Freeze - Reduces attack power
💫 Stun - Skip a turn

🦅 Flight System

✈️ Taking Flight

Click "Fly" to take to the skies! Flying lets you travel faster and access aerial encounters.

⚡ Flight Stamina

Flying consumes stamina. Your stamina bar shows how long you can stay airborne. Stamina regenerates while grounded.

🌤️ Aerial Exploration

While flying, you can spot locations from above, find sky treasures, and encounter flying enemies.

⚔️ Aerial Combat

Some enemies can only be found in the sky. Aerial battles give bonus XP! SkyWings have advantages in air combat.

🏃 Quick Travel

Flying to a new location is faster and may avoid ground encounters, but costs stamina.

💨 Tribe Flight Bonuses

SkyWing: +50% stamina, faster regen
RainWing: Camouflage while flying
SeaWing: Can dive underwater
NightWing: Better at night flights

Best for: Control playstyle

Stats: Balanced all around
Special: Nightmare - Damage + chance to stun

👑 Boss Guide

🐊 Marsh King Crocodile (Mud Kingdom)

Level: 5 | Danger: ★★☆☆☆
Abilities: Tail Swipe, Mud Trap, Regenerate
Tips: Bring antidotes. Burst damage to prevent healing.

🦂 Queen Scorpion (Sand Kingdom)

Level: 7 | Danger: ★★★☆☆
Abilities: Poison Sting, Burrow, Swarm
Tips: Stock up on antidotes. Watch for unavoidable swarm damage.

⚡ Thunderwing (Sky Kingdom)

Level: 9 | Danger: ★★★★☆
Abilities: Lightning Strike, Wind Blast, Dive Bomb
Tips: Defend against Dive Bomb! Can stun you.

🐙 Leviathan (Sea Kingdom)

Level: 8 | Danger: ★★★☆☆
Abilities: Tentacle Grab, Ink Cloud, Whirlpool
Tips: High defense boss. Use armor-piercing attacks.

🐍 Jungle Hydra (Rainforest)

Level: 10 | Danger: ★★★★☆
Abilities: Multi-Bite, Poison Breath, Regenerate
Tips: Heavy poison. Bring mega potions and antidotes.

❄️ Frost Wyrm (Ice Kingdom)

Level: 11 | Danger: ★★★★☆
Abilities: Ice Breath, Frozen Tomb, Blizzard
Tips: Bring thaw potions. Can freeze-lock you!

👁️ Darkstalker (Night Kingdom)

Level: 15 | Danger: ★★★★★
Abilities: Shadow Blast, Mind Break, Dark Resurrection
Tips: Final boss! Can heal at low HP. Max level and gear recommended.
